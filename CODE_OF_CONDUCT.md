# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project welcoming and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, constructive, and specific. Assume good intent, accept correction gracefully, and focus criticism on ideas and code rather than people.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and interactions that violate this code. Serious or repeated violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban. Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact listed in the repository profile. Reports will be reviewed in good faith and handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing the project.
